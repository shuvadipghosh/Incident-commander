# Out of Fuel Policy and Rules

Guidance for managing out of fuel scenarios:

1. **Walking to Station**:
   - Walking is only allowed if the nearest fuel station is less than 2.0 km away.
   - The weather must be clear or cloudy. If there is rain, snow, storm, or extreme temperature (below 0°C or above 38°C), walking is strictly prohibited.
   - The route must have sidewalks or safe walking shoulders. If on a major highway, walking is not permitted.

2. **Fuel Delivery Service**:
   - Standard fuel delivery supplies 2 gallons of fuel (Regular 87 or Diesel), which is sufficient to reach the nearest station.
   - Standard ETA is 30-45 minutes depending on distance and location.
   - Eligible users: Insured customers with "FUEL_DELIVERY" coverage. Non-insured customers will be charged a fee of $75.

3. **Towing Fallback**:
   - If no fuel station is within 15 km, or if the vehicle requires diesel which is currently unavailable for delivery, dispatch a flatbed tow truck to transport the vehicle to the nearest appropriate fueling facility.
