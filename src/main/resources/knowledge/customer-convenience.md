# Customer Convenience and Decision Guidelines

Rules for balancing customer convenience, cost, and time when making recommendations:

1. **Walking Distance Thresholds**:
   - Walking is a convenient option ONLY when the nearest facility (fuel station or mechanic) is within 2.0 km.
   - For distances greater than 2.0 km, walking is considered inconvenient and potentially unsafe. Recommend mobile service or towing instead.

2. **Weather Considerations**:
   - If precipitation (rain, snow, storm) is active, or if temperature is severe (below 0°C or above 38°C), walking is discouraged. Keep the customer inside the vehicle and dispatch a service vehicle.

3. **Time-Savings Preference**:
   - If a mobile technician is available within 30 minutes, prefer mobile repair/service over towing. Towing should be reserved for cases where local roadside repair is not possible or has failed.
