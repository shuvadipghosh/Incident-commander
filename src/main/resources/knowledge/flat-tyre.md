# Flat Tyre Assistance Policy

Guidance for managing flat tyre incidents:

1. **Self-Service / Tyre Change**:
   - Recommend using the vehicle's spare tyre only if the customer has a functioning spare and jack, feels comfortable changing it, and the vehicle is in a safe, flat location away from traffic.
   - If the customer is on a highway shoulder, recommend professional tyre service rather than self-service.

2. **Mobile Tyre Repair Service**:
   - Dispatch a roadside technician to change the tyre using the customer's spare.
   - If the customer does not have a spare tyre, the technician can tow the vehicle to the nearest tyre shop or attempt a plug/patch if the puncture is in the tread and under 1/4 inch.
   - Average response time is 35-50 minutes.
   - Covered under "FLAT_TYRE" policy benefits. Non-covered cost is $80.
