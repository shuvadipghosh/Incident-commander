# Accident and Collision Protocol

Guidance for managing accidents and collisions:

1. **Safety and Assessment**:
   - Verify if anyone requires medical attention. If so, immediately dispatch emergency services (911) or identify the nearest hospital.
   - If safe, move the vehicle out of the flow of traffic to the shoulder.
   - Turn off the engine and activate hazard lights.

2. **Reporting and Information**:
   - Advise the customer to contact local police to file an accident report.
   - Instruct the customer to exchange insurance information, driver's license details, and contact details with other involved parties.
   - Take photos of vehicle damage and the accident scene if safe to do so.

3. **Tow and Recovery**:
   - If the vehicle is undrivable, dispatch a tow truck.
   - Standard accident towing includes transport to a collision center or designated repair shop.
   - Covered under "TOW_TRUCK" policy benefits. Non-covered base fee is $150.
