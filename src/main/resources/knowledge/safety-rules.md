# Global Safety Rules and Guidelines

In any Roadside Assistance (RSA) scenario, the safety of the customer, passengers, and other drivers is the absolute highest priority. 

1. **High-Risk Environments**: If the vehicle is stranded in a high-speed traffic lane, on a highway shoulder, on a blind curve, or in an area with low visibility, the customer must be instructed to:
   - Turn on hazard lights immediately.
   - Exit the vehicle if safe, and move behind the highway guardrail or to a safe distance away from the roadway.
   - Do NOT attempt to perform any physical repairs (like changing a tyre) on the side of the vehicle facing active traffic.

2. **Accident Protocol**: In the event of any collision or accident:
   - First check if anyone is injured. If there are injuries, immediately call local emergency services (911).
   - If vehicles are blocking traffic but are drivable, they should be carefully moved to the shoulder.
   - If there is vehicle smoke, fire, or fuel leakage, all occupants must evacuate and stand at least 100 feet away, upwind.

3. **Extreme Weather Overrides**: 
   - Under heavy rain, snow, hail, lightning, or extreme temperatures, customers should not attempt to walk or change tyres.
   - Suggest waiting inside the vehicle with doors locked unless there is a fire/smoke risk.
