# Dead Battery Assistance Policy

Guidance for managing dead battery incidents:

1. **Jump Start Service**:
   - The primary solution for a dead battery is a jump start.
   - Dispatch a service vehicle equipped with a heavy-duty battery booster pack.
   - Standard booster service ETA is 25-40 minutes.
   - Covered under "DEAD_BATTERY" policy benefits. Non-covered cost is $60.

2. **Battery Replacement**:
   - If the jump start fails (battery will not hold charge, or battery is older than 4 years), recommend battery testing and replacement.
   - Mobile battery delivery and installation can be requested. If unavailable, the vehicle must be towed to a service center.

3. **Safety Warnings**:
   - Do not attempt to jump start a battery that is cracked, leaking fluid, or bulging, as this poses an explosion risk.
   - In wet conditions (heavy rain/lightning), use extreme caution or wait for a professional technician.
