# Service Providers, Coverage, and Cost Structure

This document outlines the standard rates, coverage eligibility, and estimated arrival times (ETA) for dispatchable services.

1. **Service ETAs**:
   - Towing Services: 40 - 60 minutes.
   - Mechanic/Tyre/Battery Jump Start: 25 - 45 minutes.
   - Fuel Delivery: 30 - 45 minutes.
   - Hospital / Ambulance: Subject to local city emergency response (typically under 10-15 minutes).

2. **Policy Coverage Matrix**:
   - **FUEL_DELIVERY**: Covers up to 2 gallons of fuel twice per year. Additional dispatches cost $75.
   - **TOW_TRUCK**: Covers flatbed towing up to 10 miles from the breakdown location to the nearest repair facility. Excess mileage is billed at $4.50/mile.
   - **FLAT_TYRE**: Covers roadside spare tyre installation. Does not cover the cost of a new tyre.
   - **DEAD_BATTERY**: Covers roadside jump start and battery health testing. Replacement batteries are billed separately.

3. **Out-of-Pocket / Non-Insured Costs**:
   - Towing base rate: $150 (includes first 10 miles).
   - Roadside service call (Flat Tyre/Battery Jump): $80.
   - Fuel Delivery service call (excluding fuel cost): $75.
