# Medical Emergency Protocol

Guidance for managing medical emergency situations:

1. **Immediate 911 Call**:
   - For any medical emergency (chest pain, shortness of breath, severe bleeding, unconsciousness, severe trauma), the primary recommendation MUST be to dial 911 immediately.
   - Do not wait for roadside assistance if a life-threatening condition exists.

2. **Nearest Hospital Tool**:
   - Use the `findNearestHospital` tool to identify the closest emergency room for information and routing.
   - Provide the hospital name, distance, and coordinates to the customer, but emphasize that an ambulance is the safest transport method for severe conditions.

3. **First Aid Guidance**:
   - Instruct the customer to stay calm, keep the patient comfortable, and not move injured persons unless there is an immediate danger of fire or explosion.
