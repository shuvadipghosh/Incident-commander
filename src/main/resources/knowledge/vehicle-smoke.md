# Vehicle Smoke / Overheating Policy

Guidance for managing smoke from engine or passenger compartments:

1. **Immediate Action**:
   - Vehicle smoke is considered a high-risk emergency. The customer must turn off the engine, evacuate all occupants immediately, and stand at least 100 feet away.
   - If there is visible flame or active fire, call 911 (Fire Department) immediately.

2. **Tow Dispatch**:
   - Under no circumstances should the customer attempt to drive a smoking vehicle.
   - Dispatch a flatbed tow truck to transport the vehicle to a certified mechanic or dealership.
   - Covered under "TOW_TRUCK" policy benefits. Non-covered cost is $150 (includes up to 10 miles).

3. **Coolant/Overheating**:
   - If the smoke is white steam from the radiator, it indicates overheating. Do NOT open the radiator cap while the engine is hot, as it can cause severe steam burns.
   - Wait for a professional technician or let it cool down for at least 45 minutes before checking coolant.
